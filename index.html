About ShoeSpot

ShoeSpot is a simple online shoe shop system created as a school project. It allows customers to browse shoes, select product options, add products to a cart and complete a purchase.

🎯 Our Purpose

The purpose of ShoeSpot is to demonstrate a basic online shopping system using HTML, CSS, JavaScript and PHP.

🛍️ Shopping Features

Customers can view products, choose a size, select a color, set the quantity, add items to the cart and purchase products.

📦 Order Tracking

After purchasing, the system records the order and automatically calculates an expected receiving date five days after purchase.

Terms and Conditions

Please read the following terms before using the ShoeSpot shopping system.

1. Product Information

Product names, descriptions and prices are provided for this school project.

2. Customer Information

Customers should provide correct name, address and contact information when completing an order.

3. Orders

Once an order is confirmed, it will be recorded in Purchase History.

4. Delivery

The expected receiving date is automatically set to five days after the purchase date.

💳 Payment Methods

ShoeSpot offers several payment options for customer convenience. Choose your preferred method during checkout.

💵 Cash on Delivery

Pay when your order is delivered. This is the simplest option if you prefer not to send payment online.

📱 GCash

Select GCash during checkout and confirm the payment instructions with the ShoeSpot owner before sending funds.

📱 Maya

Maya is available as a selectable payment option. Confirm the owner's current payment details before paying.

🏦 Bank Transfer

Customers may select bank transfer and coordinate the correct bank details with the ShoeSpot owner.

🔒 Payment Safety Notice

This website is a customer-facing shop interface and does not directly process or verify online payments. The selected payment method is saved with the order for reference. Never send payment to an unverified account or number; confirm the official details with the ShoeSpot owner first.
